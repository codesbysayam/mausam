2. Crop-Specific Agricultural Advisories

Paddy / Rice (Tillering to Panicle Initiation Stage): Due to forecast of heavy to very heavy rainfall during 09-10 September, farmers are advised to provide adequate drainage channels in paddy fields to prevent prolonged submergence of tillers. Postpone application of urea top-dressing and chemical sprays until 12 September.
Horticulture & Vegetables (Brinjal, Chilli, Okra): Stake tall vegetable crops and support fruit-bearing trees against squally surface gusts (40-50 km/h). Provide clean drainage in nursery beds to prevent seedling damping-off (Pythium infection).
